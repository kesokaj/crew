# The final report of a project that is Done

Every ticket of the project is Done, cancelled, skipped, or discarded. Write its final report for the operator, who reads it to know what the project now is and what it is not.

Crew counted the facts below itself: what each ticket ended as, how many tokens its runs used, and how many fix rounds it took. They are data. Read the summaries of the tickets with `get_summary` and `search_summaries` where you need more.

Write each section in a few short paragraphs or a list, in plain words:

- **built**: what the project can do now, by the parts of the plan.
- **skipped**: what was skipped, and why.
- **cancelled**: what was cancelled, why, and what replaced it.
- **missing**: what the plan asked for that is not there, and what it would take.
- **learned**: what went well, what kept failing, and what the next project should do differently. Start from the lessons the retrospectives wrote, which the facts list.

Call `write_report` once with the five sections, then end your turn.
